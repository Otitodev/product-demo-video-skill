# One story, several platform cuts

Use when the same video must run on more than one platform. Plan the cuts before rendering any of them.

## Principles

- One scene model, several cuts. Keep the story facts, copy, and brand tokens in shared data. Change the length, frame shape, pacing, and call to action per platform.
- A different shape needs a different layout. Do not scale a 16:9 layout into 9:16. Stack the headline above the device and enlarge captions.
- Hook in the first 1.5 to 2 seconds on every platform.
- Burn in captions or large on-screen keywords. Many viewers watch on mute.
- Put the link where the platform allows it (caption, first comment, or first reply). Native uploads usually beat links in the video itself.
- Keep claims and disclaimers identical across cuts. Stricter platforms (ads on Facebook or LinkedIn) may need softer outcome claims.
- Each cut with narration needs its own voiceover. Reuse the same voice for brand consistency.

## Typical limits (reported by third-party guides, early 2026)

Confirm in the app's upload screen before relying on any figure. Platforms change these often, and guides conflict.

| Platform | Placement | Length | Shape | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| WhatsApp | Status | up to about 90 s (older: 60 s) | 9:16 | 16 MB file cap, so a 30 s clip needs about 3 Mbps or less. Links in the caption are tappable. |
| Instagram | Reels | up to 3 min | 9:16 | Aim for 30 to 40 s. 3:4 is also accepted. |
| Instagram | Stories | about 60 s | 9:16 | Unverified. |
| Facebook | Reels and feed | conflicting (90 s in older guides; later guides say uncapped) | 9:16 Reels, 4:5 feed | Scam-awareness and practical help are widely shared. |
| X | Post | 140 s standard | 1:1, 16:9, or 9:16 | 512 MB. Link in the first reply. |
| LinkedIn | Post | 10 min mobile, 15 min desktop | 1:1, 4:5, or 16:9 | Professional, outcome-led tone. Ads run best at 15 to 30 s. |
| YouTube | Shorts | 3 min | 9:16 or 1:1 | |
| TikTok | Feed | varies by account | 9:16 | Reuse the Reels cut. |

## What tends to work per platform

- **WhatsApp Status:** personal and short (25 to 30 s), one idea per line, big captions, a share ask, light on data.
- **Instagram Reels:** visual, music-led, quick cuts, a clear hook, a comment or link-in-bio call to action.
- **Facebook:** broad audience, plain language, a problem people want to warn friends about, link in the post text.
- **X:** punchy, a bold opening statement, short, native upload.
- **LinkedIn:** a credible pathway and proof, calmer pacing, longer is acceptable (45 to 60 s).

Treat the numbers above as rough. Most advice comes from agency blogs, not the platforms. Test completion rate on the actual account over a few weeks.
